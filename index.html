GLOBAL MANDATE & MISSION

Sustainable Development Goal 05

Gender equality is not merely a fundamental human right, but a necessary foundation for a peaceful, prosperous, and sustainable world. SDG 5 provides an actionable blueprint to dismantle systemic barriers, empower marginalized voices, and build institutional equity across all sectors of society.

Why It Matters

The Foundation of Human Dignity & Prosperity

When women and non-binary individuals are afforded equal access to education, healthcare, decent work, and political representation, economies grow faster, children thrive, and communities become more peaceful and resilient.

1 in 3 women globally still experience physical or sexual violence in their lifetimes.

ACTION AREAS

Six Pillars of SDG 5 Implementation

Explore the foundational targets defined by the United Nations to achieve comprehensive gender equality by 2030.

01

Ending Discrimination

Eliminate all forms of discrimination against all women, girls, and marginalized genders everywhere in legal codes, civic institutions, and social customs.

Target 5.1 · Legal Rights
02

Eliminating Gender-Based Violence

Eradicate all forms of physical, sexual, and psychological violence against women and girls in both public and private spheres, including trafficking and sexual exploitation.

Target 5.2 · Bodily Safety
03

Ending Harmful Practices

Eliminate all harmful practices, such as child, early, and forced marriages, female genital mutilation, and dowry-related abuses that strip individuals of life choices.

Target 5.3 · Child Protection
04

Recognizing Unpaid Care Work

Recognize and value unpaid caregiving and domestic work through the provision of public services, infrastructure, social protection policies, and shared household responsibility.

Target 5.4 · Economic Equity
05

Equal Leadership Opportunities

Ensure women’s full and effective participation and equal opportunities for leadership at all levels of decision-making in political, corporate, and public life.

Target 5.5 · Governance
06

Empowerment Through Technology

Enhance the use of enabling technology, particularly information and communications technology, to promote gender equity, financial inclusion, and digital independence.

Target 5.B · Digital Access
